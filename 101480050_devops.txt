COMP3104 - Software Engineering and DevOps is a course offered at George Brown College that introduces students to the principles and practices of software development and operations.

The course covers topics such as software development life cycle (SDLC), version control, continuous integration and deployment, automation, and collaboration tools. Students gain hands-on experience with industry-standard tools and methodologies, preparing them for real-world challenges in software engineering and DevOps environments.
By learning DevOps, students develop a strong understanding of how to bridge the gap between development and operations teams. This approach encourages collaboration, communication, and shared responsibility for delivering high-quality software products.

DevOps skills are highly valued in the technology industry, as organizations seek professionals who can streamline workflows, automate repetitive tasks, and ensure reliable software delivery. The course also emphasizes the importance of continuous learning and adaptation, preparing students to thrive in fast-paced and ever-evolving environments.
