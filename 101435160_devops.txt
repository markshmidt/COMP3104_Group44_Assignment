COMP3104 – Software Engineering and DevOps is a course offered at George Brown College that introduces students to the core principles and practices of modern software development and operations.

The course explores key topics such as the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), version control, continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD), automation, and collaboration tools. Through hands-on projects and practical exercises, students gain experience with industry-standard technologies and workflows that prepare them for real-world challenges in software engineering and DevOps environments.

By studying DevOps, students learn how to effectively bridge the gap between development and operations teams. This integrated approach promotes collaboration, communication, and shared accountability, all of which are essential for delivering high-quality, reliable software products.

DevOps expertise is highly sought after in today’s technology industry. Organizations value professionals who can optimize processes, automate routine tasks, and ensure smooth, efficient software delivery. The course also emphasizes continuous learning and adaptability, equipping students with the skills and mindset needed to succeed in fast-paced, ever-evolving technical environments.
