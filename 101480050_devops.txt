COMP3104 - Software Engineering and DevOps is a course offered at George Brown College that introduces students to the principles and practices of software development and operations. The course covers topics such as software development life cycle (SDLC), version control, continuous integration and deployment, automation, and collaboration tools. Students gain hands-on experience with industry-standard tools and methodologies, preparing them for real-world challenges in software engineering and DevOps environments.
